# Silverpine Trust Bank — Company Brief

*(Fictitious institution created for demo / testing purposes only. Any resemblance to a real bank is coincidental.)*

## Overview

Silverpine Trust Bank is a retail and commercial bank headquartered in Eldermere Falls. It serves individual customers, small businesses, and mid-size enterprises through a network of physical branches, ATMs, and a full-featured digital banking platform.

## History

- **Founded:** 1978, by a group of four regional merchants who pooled capital to start a community lending cooperative called "Eldermere Mutual Trust."
- **1992:** Rebranded to Silverpine Trust Bank after receiving a full commercial banking license.
- **2003:** Opened its first out-of-region branches, expanding into three neighboring provinces.
- **2015:** Launched its digital banking platform and mobile app, "Silverpine Go."
- **2021:** Crossed 1 million active customer accounts.
- **2024:** Introduced dedicated small-business lending desks in all major branches.

- **Branches:** 68 physical branches across 4 provinces.

- **Regulator:** Chartered and supervised by the (fictitious) Central Reserve Authority of Kestria.

## Services Offered

- Personal and business savings & checking accounts
- Fixed deposits / term deposits
- Personal loans
- Car loans
- Housing (home) loans
- Credit cards
- Business and SME lending
- Wealth management and investment advisory
- Digital banking (mobile app, online transfers, bill pay)
