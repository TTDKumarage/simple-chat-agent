## 1. Personal Loans

Unsecured loans for personal expenses (medical, education, travel, home renovation, debt consolidation).

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Loan amount | $1,000 – $50,000 |
| Interest rate | 11.5% – 16.0% per annum (based on credit score) |
| Tenure | 1 to 5 years |
| Processing fee | 1.5% of loan amount (min $25) |
| Eligibility | Age 21–60, minimum steady income of $1,200/month, minimum credit score 650, at least 1 year of employment history |
| Prepayment | Allowed after 6 months; 2% prepayment charge on outstanding balance |

**Additional perks:** Approved personal loan customers get a free 12-month subscription to Silverpine's financial planning tool, "Budget Compass."
