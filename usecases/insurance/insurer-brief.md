# Havenwell Assurance Group — Company Brief

*(Fictitious institution created for demo / testing purposes only. Any resemblance to a real insurer is coincidental.)*

## Overview

Havenwell Assurance Group is a composite insurer headquartered in Brackenridge, offering life, health, motor, and home insurance to individuals and families, alongside group insurance plans for employers.

## History

- **Founded:** 1991, as "Brackenridge Mutual Assurance," a small mutual insurer focused on life cover for local tradespeople.
- **1999:** Converted from a mutual society to a shareholder-owned company and rebranded to Havenwell Assurance Group.
- **2008:** Launched its health insurance division following a merger with a regional health-cover provider, "Wellstone Health Mutual."
- **2014:** Entered the motor insurance market.
- **2019:** Launched a fully digital claims platform, "Havenwell Direct."
- **2023:** Reached 750,000 active policyholders across all lines of business.
