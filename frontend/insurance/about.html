Our story

From a mutual society to 750,000 policyholders.

Founded in Brackenridge on one belief: claims shouldn't be a fight.

History

1991 – today

1991 — Founded as "Brackenridge Mutual Assurance," a small mutual insurer focused on life cover for local tradespeople.

1999 — Converted to a shareholder-owned company and rebranded to Havenwell Assurance Group.

2008 — Launched its health insurance division after merging with regional provider "Wellstone Health Mutual."

2014 — Entered the motor insurance market.

2019 — Launched "Havenwell Direct," a fully digital claims platform.

2023 — Reached 750,000 active policyholders across all lines of business.

Mission

"Clear terms, fast claims, no surprises."

"To make protection plans that people actually understand — clear terms, fast claims, no surprises."
