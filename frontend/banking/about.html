Our story

From a four-person cooperative to 1.3 million customers.

Chartered in Eldermere Falls, built on one idea: banking shouldn't require a law degree to understand.

History

1978 – today

1978 — Founded by four regional merchants as "Eldermere Mutual Trust," a community lending cooperative.

1992 — Rebranded to Silverpine Trust Bank after receiving a full commercial banking license.

2003 — Opened its first out-of-region branches, expanding into three neighboring provinces.

2015 — Launched the "Silverpine Go" digital banking platform and mobile app.

2021 — Crossed 1 million active customer accounts.

2024 — Introduced dedicated small-business lending desks in all major branches.
